Brow mapping

How it works

Every brow appointment starts with mapping. Three measured points set the shape before any wax goes on.

  1. 1Map

    Start, arch and tail are measured from your own features.

  2. 2Shape

    Soft wax and tweezers clean up everything outside the map.

  3. 3Tint

    Hybrid tint fills sparse spots and defines the final shape.
